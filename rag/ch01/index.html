Ch1.1 What's RAG?

Large Language Models (LLMs) are trained on large but fixed corpora, which limits their ability to reason about private or up-to-date information. Fine-tuning is a way to mitigate this issue, but it is generally not suitable for recalling factual information and is relatively costly. Against this backdrop, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has gradually emerged as a mainstream and powerful mechanism. RAG uses documents retrieved from external data sources as a Knowledge Base to enhance the generation capabilities of LLMs through in-context learning. ...
