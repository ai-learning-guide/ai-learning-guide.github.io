About Me#

I am an AI engineer and have published three books in the field of AI so far. These include the simplified Chinese editions “「跟我一起学机器学习」” and “「跟我一起学深度学习」”, as well as the traditional Chinese edition “「一本書晉升深度學習世界級大師」”.

I have a deep understanding of the difficulties beginners face when entering the AI field, and I focus on helping newcomers quickly step through the door into the world of AI. The initial drafts of these materials originated from my study notes during my master’s program, including courses such as Andrew Ng’s Machine Learning course and Stanford University’s CS224N and CS231N. Since then, I have continuously refined and updated these materials for nearly 10 years.

At present, I am also writing content on RAG development based on the LangChain framework.
